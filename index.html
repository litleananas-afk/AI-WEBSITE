01 / Problem

One bad pack costs more than a stopped line.

A defective pack that reaches a customer means returns, complaints and lost trust.

A system that stops the line for no reason costs money too. You need one you can rely on.

06 / Questions

Straight answers.

What about false alarms?

It is the first thing we measure in a pilot. You see the false-alarm rate on your own line before you decide anything.

Do I need new hardware?

No new hardware. Installation takes about 2 days.

How long is a pilot?

Six weeks on a real production line.

Which defects can it catch?

Wrong or missing labels, damaged packs and foreign objects.

Who is it for?

Mid-size food and cosmetics plants that want a simpler alternative to the big vendors.
